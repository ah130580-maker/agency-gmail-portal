About MailHub by Aamiq

MailHub by Aamiq is a dedicated multi-mailbox management workspace built for teams and professionals who manage dozens or hundreds of Gmail accounts daily. Instead of juggling dozens of browser windows or losing sessions, MailHub unifies Inbox, Sent, Compose, Labels, and isolated Google Search sessions under one fast, secure workspace.

Terms, Privacy & 7-Day Refund Policy

1. Authorized Access: You agree to connect only Gmail accounts you own or have explicit authorization to manage.

2. Session Persistence & Security: Connected sessions are stored securely for workspace continuity and never shared with third parties.

3. Billing & 7-Day Grace Period: Failed renewals enter a 7-day grace period with header alerts and admin email notifications before suspension.
